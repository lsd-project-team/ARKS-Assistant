## 实际分支与待验证点

- [运输单元新增](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/TransportUnitSystem/Controls/TransportUnitEdit/FrmTransportUnitEdit.cs#L114)、[复制](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/TransportUnitSystem/Controls/TransportUnitEdit/FrmTransportUnitEdit.cs#L179)先检查弹窗OK；[删除](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/TransportUnitSystem/Controls/TransportUnitEdit/FrmTransportUnitEdit.cs#L142)要求选择、解锁及OKCancel确认；[清空](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/TransportUnitSystem/Controls/TransportUnitEdit/FrmTransportUnitEdit.cs#L286)直接清两个列表后Save，方法内没有确认条件。
- [焊点单元格编辑](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/BondPosition/UcAddBondPosition.cs#L278)使用YesNo却要求DialogResult.OK才Save；包装器[委托按钮枚举转换](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Currency/AKRSXtraMessageBox.cs#L290)并[交给XtraMessageBoxArgs](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Currency/AKRSXtraMessageBox.cs#L1295)，未见此处显式把Yes转换成OK。按标准返回值这是不可达Save的高风险条件；现场DevExpress映射未独立运行验证。双击改名的[另一条Save](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/BondPosition/UcAddBondPosition.cs#L392)是不同路径。
- [点胶名称刷新](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/DispenseSystem/Controls/Setting/EpoxyApplication/UcAddEpoxyApplication.cs#L48)自身分支Save，因此取消新增/删除后仍可能因RefreshControl触发Save。新增/删除按IsSystem2Dispense分支选择BondProgram或System1Program；[仓库弹窗返回](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/DispenseSystem/Controls/Setting/EpoxyApplication/UcAddEpoxyApplication.cs#L152)在选中有效对象后顺序调用仓库、System1Program、BondProgram三次Save，再刷新。
- [焊后新增](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/PostBond/UcAddPostBond.cs#L93)本方法只在OK时刷新，没有直接Save；[删除](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/PostBond/UcAddPostBond.cs#L109)确认后顺序保存检查仓库与BondProgram；[管理](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/PostBond/UcAddPostBond.cs#L140)打开仓库，并传递[PR删除回调](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/BondSystem/Controls/Setting/PostBond/UcAddPostBond.cs#L64)，弹窗返回后重新并入名称并Save。没有跨仓库原子性证据。
