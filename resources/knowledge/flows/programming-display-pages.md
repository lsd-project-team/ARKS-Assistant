## 配置展示页的数据流

- [构造函数](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L25)调用RefreshControl；[75–79行](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L75)把吸嘴架、顶针架对象绑定到两个GridControl；[85–87行](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L85)从程序对象读取三个名称并赋给Label。
- [树控件配置](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L44)设Editable=false；[刷新树](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L121)根据晶圆分配对象创建页面内Node列表并添加TreeList；[焦点事件](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcConfiguration2.cs#L111)只修改页面内两个索引。
- 宿主[Save按钮](../../trunk/AKRS.ZX2200/Infrastructure/Controls/Feature/EditProgram/UcEditProductProgram/UcEditProductProgramming.cs#L1066)可选调用IProgrammingControl.Confirm后显示“保存成功”；这些主类均未直接声明该接口，因此该按钮不触发它们的Confirm。四种其他非接口页面通过各自事件另行保存，不能用按钮消息判断其是否写入。

**后续边界：**GridControl的数据绑定是否允许用户编辑、控件事件及继承/第三方行为未做设备UI验收；本报告没有把“没有直接Save”提升为“绝不修改对象/文件”。源文件和Designer的SHA256见[机器可读证据](programming-display-pages.json)。
